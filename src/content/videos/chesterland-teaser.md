Some stories never make it past the teaser. This one still deserves to be seen.

Chesterland was a half-hour dramedy set in the blue-collar belt outside Cleveland, following a hard-working, middle-class Italian family through the struggles and the wins that make it all worth it.

I’m from Ohio, so this one hit close to home. These are the people I grew up around: the ones who clock in, show up, and keep the family going no matter what.
