I didn’t go to film school. I went to film camp, put everything I had into a camera, and figured out the rest along the way. So ACCEL Schools’ whole philosophy landed with me right away: it’s not where you start, it’s where you’re going.

ACCEL partners with communities across the country to build schools where learning is tailored to each kid, for students and families of every background. We made two spots for them, a :30 and a :60, focused on the students and the futures they’re building.

Every kid deserves a story about where they’re headed. These were a joy to tell.
