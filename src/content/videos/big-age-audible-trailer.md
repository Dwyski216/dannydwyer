Cedric the Entertainer, Jennifer Lewis, Niecy Nash-Betts. A room full of comedy royalty, and I got to direct a really fun promo for Big Age, streaming on Audible.

The hardest part of the job was calling "moving on." Every take got funnier, and every time I pulled us to the next setup I was leaving gold on the floor. Best problem I've ever had.
