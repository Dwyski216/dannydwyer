My best friend Nate Fish is the King of Jewish Baseball. His lifelong dream was to take batting practice in the streets of New York City, and he wanted me there to film it.

So we did. There are no words for how much fun that day was. Then pro ballplayers started sharing it all over social media, and it took on a life of its own.

Nate, thanks for being you, and for being a great friend.
