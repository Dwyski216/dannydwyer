When you get to shoot the daughter of Diana Ross, you know it's going to be a good day.

Tracee was a dream, a ball of warmth who had the whole crew laughing between takes. She also spent most of the day in a hot tub, so nobody was complaining.

Thank you for making it easy. I hope we get to do it again.
