Kimpton named this hotel after theta waves, the brain waves of deep relaxation. Then they put it in the Theater District. Bold move.

My job was to capture both sides: a hotel built to slow you down, and a neighborhood that never will. So we filled it with young couples, handed them the keys, and I chased them with the camera from Café Otto in the lobby all the way up to Bar Sprezzatura, the Positano-style rooftop, spritzes in hand and the skyline wrapped around them.

A calm hotel, fun talent, and a rooftop that turned every setup into a chill party. Theta waves, our way.
