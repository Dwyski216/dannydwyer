Cotton is everywhere, which is exactly why nobody thinks about it.

For BBDO, on behalf of America's cotton industry, we traveled across the country to sit down with fabric, fashion, and textile designers and learn how cotton is woven into their work and their lives (pun fully intended). From studio to studio and state to state, we saw how one humble fiber quietly touches almost everything we wear, sleep in, and live with.
