Sue Aikens hadn’t been in a city in over 20 years. She lives alone at Kavik River Camp, 80 miles from the nearest road. Then Nat Geo brought her to New York.

For Life Below Zero, we followed Sue as she took it all in: The Highline, The Garment district, a farmers market, and a slew of others. We shot everything in the moment. It was incredible what we take for granted living in a major city with everything available at your fingertips. At times it often asked if we didn't have it better either. Who's to say. What I did take away was to appreciate what is in front of you in the moment. 

Life. 

Thank you, Nat Geo and Sue, for letting me see NYC through new eyes.
