A studio, a table full of Shinola watches, and a lot of room to play.

We experimented with motion and light, chasing the glint a sweeping hand throws off and the way steel holds a highlight. Watches are about time, so every move had to feel precise and unhurried. Our editor nailed the rhythm and effects.
