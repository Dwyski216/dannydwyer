Trail dust in the morning, client lunch by afternoon.

We took the Chukka up into Malibu Canyon, deep in the Santa Monica Mountains, to show a boot that can handle both. As a cinematographer, you dream about a location like this: sage-green brush and sun-baked earth, all sitting in the same family of tones as the boot. I didn't have to force a single color. The canyon just handed it over.

When a place and a product share a palette, the image doesn't just look good. It looks inevitable.
