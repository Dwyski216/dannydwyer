Half dress shoe, half sneaker, and it refuses to pick a side.

The Crossover lives in two worlds at once: the polish of a leather dress shoe on top, and the comfort of a sneaker underneath. The job was to shoot both sides of that split personality without letting either one win.

It’s the shoe for the guy who doesn’t want to choose. Turns out, neither did the camera.
