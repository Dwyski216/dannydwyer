Brendan collects coins, and he's not casual about it.

In Pt. 3, his Pixel helps him learn about each coin he owns, then heads outside with him to explore the natural world. Kids ask a hundred questions a day. Brendan finally has something that can keep up with him. Had so much fun throughout this shoot with him, he was so inquisitive on the gear we used and was fun showing him how everything worked.

Being there for those answers might be my favorite part of the whole series
