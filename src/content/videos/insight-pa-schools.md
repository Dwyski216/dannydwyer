How do you film a school that doesn’t have a building?

Insight PA is a full-time online public school for K–12 students across Pennsylvania. Class happens at the kitchen table, on the road, anywhere there’s an internet connection, with certified teachers on the other side of the screen. So instead of hallways and lockers, the camera went where the learning actually happens.

Thirty seconds to make a family feel what that freedom looks like. That’s the whole job.
