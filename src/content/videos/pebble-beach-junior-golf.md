Pebble Beach is one of the most famous places in golf, and one of the hardest to get into. Since 1988, Pebble Beach Junior Golf has been opening those gates to local kids, ages 6 to 17, across the Monterey Peninsula.

Clinics, tournaments on legendary courses, and over a million dollars in college scholarships, all built around a motto they live by: “scuffs over scores.” The game, the rules, and the respect that comes with them.

The sizzle had to carry 38 years of history and still feel as young as the kids swinging the clubs. Old-school course, new-school energy.
