A dress shoe on a skateboard sounds like a bad idea. That was the point.

We took the Swiftknit down to the beach and let it ride the boardwalk. It isn't a skate shoe, and it doesn't pretend to be. It's a dress shoe you can actually move in: hop on a board, cut through the crowd, and get where you're going without your feet feeling like bricks.

Sun on the pavement, salt in the air, and a shoe that keeps up with all of it.
