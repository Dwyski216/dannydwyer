Some people need an introduction. Jane Fonda is not one of them.

We sat down one-on-one for Harper's Bazaar, and for a while the job almost disappeared. It was just her voice, her story, and a lifetime of it filling the room. Decades of films, fights, and reinventions came out in her own words, with the kind of candor you only get from someone who's stopped caring what anyone thinks.

My job was to light her with the respect that story deserves, then get out of the way. It felt less like a shoot and more like sitting in on history. An honor, full stop.
