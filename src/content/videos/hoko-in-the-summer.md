The song is called “In The Summer.” The Pacific didn’t get the memo.

We shot at Point Dume in Malibu on 16mm, with director Mama Hot Dog steering the whole thing straight into the ’70s: warm color, soft grain, and big, fast zooms every chance we got. No crystals this time. Just film, the coastline, and a camera that wouldn’t sit still.

Then these guys walked right into that freezing water anyway. Summer on screen, goosebumps off it. That’s commitment.

Another one with Mama Hot Dog, and another reminder why I always say yes.
